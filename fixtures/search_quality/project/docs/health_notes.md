# Health Notes

## Cholesterol

Cholesterol measurements should be taken annually. LDL cholesterol levels above 160 mg/dL indicate elevated cardiovascular risk.

## Blood Pressure Ranges

| Category | Systolic | Diastolic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal | 120 | 80 |
| Elevated | 130 | 80 |
| High | 140 | 90 |
